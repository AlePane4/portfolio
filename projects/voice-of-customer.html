Voice of Customer — mining 50,000+ verbatims for a car delivery service

Open-text feedback is where customers explain the why behind the scores — but nobody can read thousands of comments. I built an NLP + GenAI pipeline that turns raw verbatims into a journey-level synthesis of pain points and strengths, used to prioritise the CX roadmap.

Data
50K+ verbatims · surveys, app reviews, support notes
Methods
Embeddings, topic modelling (BERTopic + Louvain), sentiment, LLM synthesis
Stack
Python · BERTopic · LLM API · Power BI

Approach

A five-step pipeline, with the LLM used at the end — for synthesis and labelling — on top of a solid, reproducible NLP foundation. Every AI-generated insight is traceable back to the underlying verbatims.

Collect & clean Merge sources, deduplicate, remove PII, language detection and translation.
Embed Sentence embeddings to represent each verbatim in semantic space.
Topic modelling BERTopic clustering + Louvain community detection on the verbatim similarity graph → 24 granular topics, merged into 9 business themes.
Sentiment & journey tagging Each verbatim scored for sentiment and mapped to a journey stage.
GenAI synthesis LLM summarises each topic × stage cell into plain-language insights, with representative quotes.

Output 4 — GenAI synthesis: pain points vs what works

Example of the executive one-pager generated by the pipeline: the top pain points and strengths, each with its share of mentions and a plain-language summary written by the LLM and validated by a human.

Top pain points
  • 4-hour delivery windows are too broad Customers must block half a day off work; the #1 driver of detractor scores. ↳ 19% of negative mentions
  • Arrival notifications come too late "Driver is arriving" alerts often fire under 10 minutes before arrival. ↳ 14% of negative mentions
  • Rescheduling requires calling support No self-service reschedule in app; long hold times amplify frustration. ↳ 11% of negative mentions
  • Paper-based handover documents Signing physical forms feels outdated and slows down an otherwise great moment. ↳ 8% of negative mentions
What works
  • Drivers are courteous and well-prepared Named praise for drivers is the single most frequent positive theme. ↳ 24% of positive mentions
  • Cars arrive clean and as described Condition on arrival consistently matches or exceeds expectations. ↳ 18% of positive mentions
  • Live map tracking builds trust When tracking works, customers describe the wait as "stress-free". ↳ 15% of positive mentions
  • Transparent booking & pricing No hidden fees; booking flow described as fast and clear. ↳ 12% of positive mentions

Impact

The journey map reframed the CX roadmap: investment shifted from the booking flow (already strong) to scheduling and notifications, where two thirds of negative feedback concentrated.
Reading time for the CX team dropped from days of manual tagging per wave to an automated refresh, with every insight traceable to source verbatims.
The framework is reusable: same pipeline, new data source — it was later extended to call-centre transcripts with no structural changes.
